Scope: the whole ras.ruchi site (home, collection cards, study sheets, practice, contact). Visitor mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.

Audience: people who meet Shruchi's work (peers, collaborators, friends, buyers of her Yellow Dwelling designs). Job: see the full range, feel the hand behind it, remember one story. Action: lift swatches, open a card, follow on Instagram, write to her.

Proof/content: 92 colourways of 64 Yellow Dwelling designs across Rasaa, Entwine, Ratna, Pause and earlier seasons; 12 Behance studies in her own words. Constraints: no invented claims, no fake codes, email is a placeholder (xyz@gmail.com).

Memorable moment: lifting a swatch off the card and seeing that exact cloth hanging in a room.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Her work is shown the way a mill shows cloth: cut, pinked swatches on a shade card, every design in its colourways, its story printed beside it. Refuses the project-tile portfolio grid and the cream-paper craft site.

OWN-WORLD: Grey mill board ground (charcoal board in dark mode); square pinked-edge swatches glued on with a soft shadow; one printed colour band per collection (crimson Ratna, herb green Rasaa, mist blue Entwine, mustard Pause, indigo earlier seasons); Mona Sans with condensed caps for printed labels and measured hex shade codes, wide heavy weights for card headers; zero radius; a punched ring hole on every card.

STORY: Visitors see her range at a glance, lift a swatch to see the cloth in a room, open a card to read a collection or study, and leave knowing one hand made all of it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One wide shade card. "ras.ruchi" printed huge across its header; a 17-word introduction at left; a room photograph window at right; along the bottom a row of ten real swatches from five collections, each labelled design, colourway and measured hex. Lifting a swatch swaps the window. Navigation is the card's printed top rule.

FORM: The Shade Card, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (taken as Impeccable's pick by the user). seed: none (unavailable). The direction roll ran and printed no SEED REFERENCE line; this CLI version has no --check-seed to corroborate it. Signature interaction: lift a swatch (lift, tilt, shadow, room crossfade; siblings dim on collection cards). Motion grammar: one dealt-chips entrance on load, ease-out exponential, view-transition morph from a collection card to its page header; static under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
