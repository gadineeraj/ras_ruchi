# Your Studio: a guide

The Studio is where you change your website. What you see in it is your real site, so you can click straight onto anything and change it. Nothing goes live until you press **Publish**, and every earlier version is kept.

## Opening the Studio

1. Open this folder and double-click **Open Studio**.
   - The very first time, your Mac may say it can't check the file. If so, right-click **Open Studio**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   - The first time also takes a minute or two to get ready.
2. Your browser opens the Studio. A small Terminal window opens too. Leave it open while you work, and close it when you're done.

The Studio needs Node.js, a free program. If it isn't installed, the Studio opens its download page: install the **LTS** version, then double-click **Open Studio** again.

## The screen

- **Top bar:**
  - which page you're editing (click its name to switch)
  - Undo and Redo
  - **Desktop / Tablet / Phone**, to check every screen size
  - whether your work is saved
  - **Find** (or ⌘K)
  - **Preview**
  - **Publish**
- **The page in the middle:** your site, exactly as visitors see it.
- **The settings on the right:** whatever you clicked. If you haven't clicked anything, it shows the page's settings: its name, web address and how it appears on Google.
- **The rail on the left:**
  - **Pages:** every page, collection page and study. Make new ones here.
  - **Add:** blocks to drag onto the page, and the section designs.
  - **Layers:** the page as a list of blocks, handy for finding small ones.
  - **Photos:** all your photos. Add new ones by dropping them here.
  - **Collections:** your collections, designs and colourways, and their swatches.
  - **Site:** your name, email, menu, footer, colours and share image.
  - **History:** saved versions and earlier copies, to go back in time.
  - **Help:** these instructions, inside the Studio.

## Everyday things

**Change words.** Click any heading or paragraph on the page and type. Select words to make them bold or italic, or to turn them into a link.

**Add a section.**
1. Move your mouse over the page, between two sections. A round **+ Add section** button appears.
2. Press it and choose a design.
3. It lands right there, filled with placeholder words. Click them and type your own.

**Add a single block.** Open **Add** on the left. Drag a block onto the page to the exact spot, or press the **+** on a block to put it after the one you've selected.

**Change a photo.**
1. Click the photo, then use the panel on the right: **Replace** chooses another from your library or uploads a new one.
2. Click the most important part of the photo (a motif, a face) so it stays in view when the photo is cropped.
3. Write a short description; it helps people who can't see the image.

**Move, copy, hide or delete.**
- Click a block. A dark bar appears above it with:
  - the arrows, to move it up or down
  - the eye, to hide it from the published site (it stays here, faded, until you show it again)
  - copy
  - delete
- Deleted something by mistake? Press **Undo**.

**Change sizes, spacing and colours.** Click a block and look on the right. **Layout** sets how wide it is and how much space sits above it. More choices (which screens show it, a gentle entrance, a name links can jump to) are under **More**. Colours come from your palette in **Site**.

**Make a mood board.** Add a **Free canvas** (in **Add**, or the **Mood board** section design). Then press **Arrange on a big board** on the right. You can:
- drag photos, words and swatches anywhere
- resize and turn them
- overlap them

On phones the whole board shrinks to fit, so it never breaks.

**Add a page.**
1. Open **Pages** and press **New page**.
2. Name it and choose a starting point: a blank page, a text page, a gallery, a study, or a collection page.
3. New pages start as **drafts**: they stay off the published site until you switch Draft off in the page's settings.
4. To put a page in the menu, use its **…** menu in Pages and choose **Show in menu**.

**Add a collection, design or colourway.**
1. Open **Collections**.
2. For each colourway, choose its close-up photo and its photo in a room.
3. Then **Cut a new swatch**: click the centre of the cloth and choose how much of the photo to use.

The swatch's colour code is measured from the cloth for you.

**Check every screen.** Use **Desktop / Tablet / Phone** at the top while you work. **Preview** opens the page in a new tab, as visitors see it.

## Publishing

1. Press **Publish**. The Studio checks your site first (links that go nowhere, empty blocks, photos without descriptions) and lists what changed since you last published. Press **Go to it** beside any item to jump straight there.
2. If you like, press **See the finished site first** to build it exactly as it will go online.
3. Press **Publish**. GitHub Desktop opens:
   1. Write a few words in **Summary** (for example "New photos for Ratna").
   2. Press **Commit to main**.
   3. Press **Push origin**.
4. About two minutes later, your site is live.

## If something goes wrong

- **Undo** (⌘Z) steps back through your changes on each page.
- **History** keeps:
  - **saved versions** of the whole site (save one before a big change: "Before the festive update")
  - **earlier copies** of the page you're on, saved automatically as you work
  - **deleted pages**, ready to bring back
- **Publish** has **Discard changes since the last publish**, which puts everything back the way it was when you last published. Today's work is still kept in History.
- If the top bar says **Couldn't save**, click it to try again. If the Studio stops responding, close the Terminal window and double-click **Open Studio** again. Nothing you saved is lost.

## Good to know

- **The fonts and the look are fixed.** Rozha One for titles, Bodoni Moda italic for taglines, Jost for text, square corners, card stock and mill board. That keeps every new page looking like it belongs.
- **Photos:** JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF are all fine. iPhone photos in HEIC need exporting as JPEG first (Photos app: File > Export).
- **Videos:** a YouTube, Vimeo or Instagram link works best. Uploaded clips should be under 60 MB.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:**
  - ⌘K find anything
  - ⌘S save now
  - ⌘D copy a block
  - ⌘↑ ⌘↓ move it
  - Esc deselect
  - ⌘Z undo
